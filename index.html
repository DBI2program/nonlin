ERC RESEARCH PROJECT · RADBOUD UNIVERSITY

Understanding cognition through large-scale brain interactions.

NONLIN investigates how distributed brain areas interact in complex, non-linear and nonseparable ways to give rise to cognitive processes.

Explore the research → About NONLIN
ABOUT NONLIN

How do distributed brain areas interact to give rise to cognition?

The project develops new ways to measure and understand interactions across distributed brain areas, with particular emphasis on dynamics that cannot be adequately described as linear or separated into independent components.
